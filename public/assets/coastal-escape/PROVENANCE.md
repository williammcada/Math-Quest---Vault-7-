# Coastal Escape narrative assets

Original project artwork generated with the built-in image generation tool on 1 October 2026; exported to WebP for the game. No third-party photographs or game assets sampled. Full prompts: fictional occupied 1940s island; cinematic painterly teal and amber; no readable text, national flags, historic insignia, or graphic violence.

- cover.webp: silver prototype propeller aircraft with navy wings, mechanics and warm lights in a concealed coastal hangar; cliffside sea view, distant lighthouse and evacuation boats.
- radio.webp: adult woman radio operator with headphones and valve radio in a lighthouse; coastal chart, compass and rolled research plans; stormy water and distant searchlights.
- ending.webp: silver/navy prototype on a sheltered strip at dawn; crew with radio/document case; civilian boats reaching a harbor.

Original music is synthesized by `scripts/coastal-audio.py`, using a quiet minor-key chord/arpeggio ambient cue and a brighter melodic finale. No commercial composition or sampled recording used. Existing aerial flight/boss music and sprites retain their original manifest/provenance.
