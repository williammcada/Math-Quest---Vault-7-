JOURNEY — THURSDAY 8 OCTOBER PLAYTEST

1. Download and unzip Journey-Thursday-Playtest.zip on your computer.
2. Double-click Journey-Solo-Practice-stage8.html. Open it in Chrome or Edge.
3. Wait for Start solo practice, choose a hero and upgrade, then Start.
4. Move with arrows/WASD; J attacks, K jumps, L uses magic. Touch controls also exist.
5. Test every hero, three lives, three-minute run, pickups, four environments and Nezha.
6. Test Music and Effects separately. Pause freezes the solo run. Changing tabs pauses it.
7. Finish / choose hero lets you start a fresh practice. Nothing saves to classroom reports.

The single HTML has everything embedded and needs no internet or Cloudflare.
It is a large file; allow time for artwork to load. On iPhone/iPad, Files/Quick Look
may show a preview without running JavaScript. That is not a game failure:
use a real browser-served copy for mobile testing. Desktop double-click is tested.
