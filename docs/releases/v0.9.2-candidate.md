## Changes

- Shared server-authoritative equipment selection: one slot after required mathematics, one additional slot per completed optional preparation block, maximum three distinct resources. Recommendations, readiness, confirmation, continuation, and stored loadouts use that entitlement. Preparation records preserve the academic attempt history. Existing teacher Extend activity remains available.
- All six equipment resources flow into the respective action simulation. Vault supports combined scanner/toolkit/cloak loadouts with individual consumption state; Nightfall preserves carbine, ammunition pouch, and vest behavior.
- Shared fraction checking validates numeric value and raw submitted form, rejects equivalent unreduced final answers with useful feedback, preserves the student's entry, and records incorrect first attempts. Explicit specified-denominator tasks retain a documented representation exception, validated on import.
- Nightfall car contact/projectiles trigger a twelve-second alarm and enemy diversion, with garage ingress taking priority until entry. Battery collection triggers eight garage attackers and immediate doorway breach.
- Separate sporting-goods survivor encounter, two breakable entry windows, permanently locked front door, and eight guards at every threat setting. Independent eight-shell shotgun pickup, seven-pellet thirty-degree spread, separate ammunition, and touch/F switching. Snapshot recovery retains ammunition and encounter state.
- Authored car atlas rendering, additional interior decoration, and reproducible procedural sound effects. Alarm sound follows simulation state; sound failure does not block play.
- Landscape phone layout, portrait rotation prompt, touch controls, pause/recovery hooks, and practice restart. Phone layout received source inspection only; no visual or physical-device pass is claimed.
- Vault's 180-second active limit and authoritative 300-second team window are retained. Unstarted/late clients do not prevent shared completion.
