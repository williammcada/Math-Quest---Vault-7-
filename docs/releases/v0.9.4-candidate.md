- First vote opens a separate early-rescue stage. Persisted phase/run/attempt IDs, one 300-second window, coherent start/escort checkpoints, unlimited downed retries, per-student terminal records and idempotent shared Gate 2 transition.
- Service alarm closes missing, loading or disconnected players without an acknowledgement. Teacher pause adjusts the deadline once; teacher may close the stage. Extensions skip live rescue teams.
- Compact south-road/alley/north-road layout; route-specific clinic/Depot Garage interiors; 5/10/5 starting zombies; pistol/15 rounds, exactly two pickups, one alarm and one gas tank. Reuses existing simulation/rendering/controls/audio. Noncombatant follows the player's traversed path and does not participate in damage or collision.
- Short dialogue, shared timer, retry/waiting panels, chapter-specific assisted route and owner practice. Gate 2 now acknowledges the completed crew rescue and checks the still-needed battery/repair plan.
- Early engagement outcomes/retries are exported separately from academic evidence and final-city runs. No equipment reward, imported gear, narrative death or final-run state leakage.
